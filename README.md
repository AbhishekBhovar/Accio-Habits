HP54 — FIXED CAMPAIGN / READINESS / TEXT-ADVENTURE PROTOTYPE
================================================================
This build directly addresses the HP52/HP53 feedback:
• Campaign uses the approved cinematic mountain artwork as the visual hero.
• Year 1 is active when the player is in Year 1; future years stay locked.
• No chapter-level Journey map. Campaign shows only training, trials and boss encounters.
• Every completed mapped habit generates Readiness XP immediately.
• Readiness cards show actual XP progress, next capability, and today's category contribution.
• Four pillars only: Magic, Combat & Skills, Knowledge, Allies & Arsenal.
• Read / Study feeds Knowledge.
• Battle is a multi-scene choose-your-action text adventure.
• Choices can succeed, fail, or require specific Readiness ranks.
• Each encounter displays its own XP gate and tells the player what to train next.
• Books 1–2 only remain the deliberate gameplay prototype.
• Old level-up rewards remain hidden; readiness awards remain the player-facing progression.
